## National Cyber League Fall 2026 Season

VISI will be competing as a team in the upcoming NCL Fall 2026 season! The National Cyber League (NCL) is a cybersecurity competition that prepares students for a cyber career.

In the NCL Games, students work to solve challenges such as identifying hackers from forensic data, pentesting and auditing vulnerable websites, recovering from ransomware attacks, and more. Participate in the season to track your skills growth with an NCL Scouting Report or represent your school in the national Cyber Power Rankings. Whether you are building your skills or competing for fun, NCL has something for you.

Registration is open **Aug. 17 - Oct 9** and costs **$45** to participate. Late registration is **Oct. 10 - 13** with a cost increase to **$55**.
