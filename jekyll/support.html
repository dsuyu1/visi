Clinic help is free for local small businesses and nonprofits. Don't include passwords, account details, or descriptions of weaknesses. We'll set up a meeting first.

Never send your password. We verify who you are before changing any account or access.

Thanks for reporting. Describe the issue and where it is. Don't send exploit code or data you accessed. We'll reply with a private way to share details. Please only test systems VISI owns.

We reply within {{ site.support.response_goal }}. Replies come by email from Jira Service Management, and you can track the request at the VISI help center.
