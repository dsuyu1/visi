The Vaquero Information Security Initiative (VISI) is a cybersecurity student organization and 501(c)(3) nonprofit based in the Rio Grande Valley. We help students build practical skills, find mentorship, and give back to their communities.

## Our mission

Produce cybersecurity professionals and give back. Members take initiative through research, projects, conferences, and connections with other people in security.

## Our pillars

- **Devotion.** We protect the communities we come from. When we learn something, we share it.
- **Dedication.** We aim to produce quality work.
- **Curiosity.** We ask questions, explore, and share what we find.
- **Honor.** We act honestly and within the law.
