The most common question after a workshop is where to practice. The answer is a home lab, and the version that works is smaller than most people expect.

## The minimum setup

A hypervisor on the machine you already own, one Linux VM, and one deliberately vulnerable target VM on an isolated network. That is enough to practice scanning, exploitation, log review, and most of the labs we run.

Keep the lab network isolated. A vulnerable VM bridged onto your home network is a vulnerable machine on your home network.

## What to add next

- A second target running a different operating system.
- Somewhere to ship logs so you can practice searching them.
- Snapshots, so you can break things and roll back in seconds.

## Things that will bite you

Disk space goes first, then RAM. Snapshot before every experiment, not after. And write down what you configured — in six weeks you will not remember which setting made the thing work.
