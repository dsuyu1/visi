Capture the flag is the fastest way to find out which parts of security you actually enjoy. We booked a room, ordered food, and worked through picoCTF as a group.

*This post is placeholder copy while we move the real content over.*

## How we organized it

We split into pairs by category — web, forensics, cryptography, reversing — and put a shared doc on the projector so nobody burned an hour on a challenge that was already solved. Pairs rotated categories halfway through so everyone touched something unfamiliar.

## What went well

Forensics was the best entry point for people who had never done this before. The challenges reward patience and a hex editor more than prior knowledge, and the first flag gets found within about ten minutes.

## What we got stuck on

Binary exploitation, predictably. We got through the introductory challenges and stalled the moment stack layout mattered. That is now a workshop on the calendar rather than a gap we pretend does not exist.

## Advice for your first one

Read the hints. They are not cheating — they are the curriculum. And write down what you tried, because the writeup afterward is where most of the learning actually lands.
