Not every meeting needs a lab. We closed out the semester with a social, which is how most people end up finding a project or a study partner.

## Who showed up

A good mix of returning members and people who had never been to a meeting. Several first-years came in expecting a lecture and left on a CTF team.

## What came out of it

Three things that are now actually on the calendar:

- A study group for members working toward their first certification.
- A standing lab night for anyone building out a home lab.
- A writeup review session, so drafts get feedback before they go on the site.

## Next semester

Meetings pick back up in the fall, and the first one is an intro session aimed at people with no background in security. Bring someone who has been curious but has not come yet.
