The Vaquero Information Security Initiative is a student organization at UTRGV for people who want to work in information security, plus anyone curious enough to find out whether they do.

*This post is placeholder copy while we move the real content over — meeting times and links will be filled in before the semester starts.*

## Who this is for

Every major. The club runs from an assumption that you know nothing about security yet, because most members started that way. If you can install software on your own laptop, you can follow along at your first meeting.

## What meetings look like

Most sessions are hands-on: a short introduction, then a lab everyone works through together. Others are recaps, guest talks, or CTF practice. Nobody is graded and nobody is quizzed.

## How to get involved

1. Come to a meeting. That is the entire onboarding process.
2. Join the group chat so you hear about sessions and competitions.
3. Pick something to work on — a CTF team, a home lab, a certification, a writeup.

Questions before you show up? Use the [contact page](/contact) and an officer will get back to you.
