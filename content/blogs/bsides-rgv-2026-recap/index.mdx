## Why local beats large for students

At a large conference you are one badge in twenty thousand. At a community conference the person who gave the talk is standing by the coffee twenty minutes later and will happily answer a question from a student. Several members left with contacts at organizations hiring in the Valley.

## Talks that stuck with us

The sessions that landed hardest were the ones about incident response in organizations without a security team — which is most organizations around here. The problems were resource problems, not exotic ones.

## Going next year

We plan to send a larger group and submit a talk of our own. If you have a project, a writeup, or a research idea, a community conference is a realistic first venue. Bring it to a meeting and we will help you shape the proposal.
